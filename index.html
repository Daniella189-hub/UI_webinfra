About

The problems

Medicine labels are long, technical and printed in tiny text. Many people never read them, so they miss warnings, side effects and interactions that matter.


What Labelens does

Labelens finds the official label for a medicine and splits it into short sections (warnings, side effects, interactions,dosage and uses), so you can find what you need quickly.

How to use it

  1. Type the medicine name in the search box. The generic name, such as paracetamol, works best
  2. Choose which section to read, or view them all
  3. Read the label, then contact a partner hospital or pharmacist if you have questions.

Where the data comes from

Labels come from the openFDA Drug Label API. These are United States labels, so some brand names used in Rwanda may not appear, and label may differ from the product you hold.

Hospitals details come fromOpenStreetMap

Please note

Labelens gives information only. It is not medical advice. label data can be out of dat. Always ask a doctor or pharmacist befor etaking, stopping or combining medicines.

Privacy

Labelens does not store your searches or any personal information

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Labelens instead of asking a doctor?

Try the generic name instead of the brand name. The labels come from the US, so some local brands are missing.

Why can't I find my medicine?

NO. Labelens helps you read the official label. It cannot tell you what is right for your health.

Why does a section say "Not listed"?

Some labels do not include every section. This is a gap in the label data, not an error in the app.
